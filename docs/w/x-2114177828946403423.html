Image prompt:

A hyper-realistic photograph of a tiny collectible figurine of a Japanese sumo wrestler, about 10 cm tall, standing on a miniature clay sumo ring (dohyo) with a straw rope border, placed on a wooden tabletop. The figurine has an oversized, photorealistic head with a chubby round face, a neat oiled topknot (chonmage), wide startled eyes and lips puckered into a surprised "oh!" expression. His small chibi body is round and plump, wearing only a dark navy silk mawashi belt with hanging sagari strings, bare feet planted wide.

A giant real human index finger enters the frame from the right side and pokes into the wrestler's big bare belly, the soft skin visibly dimpling and jiggling around the fingertip. The scale contrast between the huge finger and the tiny figurine is the focus of the image.

Background: a softly blurred traditional sumo arena, with the hanging shrine-style roof (tsuriyane), purple tassels, warm spotlights and out-of-focus crowd seating, creating creamy bokeh.

Macro tabletop photography, shallow depth of field, figurine and fingertip in sharp focus, soft warm key light from the upper left, subtle rim light, natural skin texture, realistic materials, playful and humorous mood. Vertical 2:3 composition with the figurine placed slightly left of center, eye-level camera angle.

Video prompt:

Use the input image as the first frame. A tiny photorealistic sumo wrestler figurine with an oversized head, oiled topknot, navy mawashi with hanging sagari strings, standing barefoot on a miniature clay sumo ring on a wooden tabletop, inside a softly blurred sumo arena with a hanging shrine roof, purple tassels and warm bokeh lights. A giant human index finger is already touching his bare belly from the right.

0–1.5s: The giant finger pulls back slightly, then pokes his round belly. The soft belly dimples inward and jiggles, his cheeks puff up comically and his eyes bulge in surprise. His body bounces back to its normal shape.

1.5–3s: The finger pokes his belly a second time, a little harder. His cheeks inflate even more, his eyes go wide and his lips purse into a funny shocked "oh!". He wobbles on his bare feet, the sagari strings swinging.

3–4.5s: The giant hand rises and gently presses down on top of his head with a fingertip, squishing his soft rubbery body down into a flat round pancake shape on the clay ring, playful cartoon squash. Hold the flattened shape for a brief beat.

4.5–5.5s: He springs back up into his original shape like a soft rubber toy, with a quick elastic overshoot and settle.

5.5–7s: He shakes himself off, tugs his mawashi back into place with both hands, then turns his face toward the giant finger and gives an exaggerated angry glare, puffed cheeks and deeply furrowed eyebrows. Hold on his funny angry expression until the end.

Style: photorealistic miniature figurine, soft rubber skin texture, physically believable belly deformation combined with playful cartoon squash-and-stretch, expressive face, macro tabletop photography, shallow depth of field, warm arena lighting with soft bokeh, natural human hand movement, cute, funny and harmless mood.

Camera: locked-off shot matching the input image framing, vertical 2:3, eye-level macro view, no camera movement, the sumo wrestler stays fully visible on the miniature ring throughout.

Keep the sumo wrestler's face, topknot, body shape and navy mawashi consistent throughout the entire video.
