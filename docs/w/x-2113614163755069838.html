Create a 15-second, 16:9 photorealistic sci-fi battle with the explosive acceleration, rapid reversals, and aerial combat rhythm of Dragon Ball Z, filmed with blockbuster action-movie cinematography.

The sequence starts immediately with Oni Samurai being blasted through buildings, develops into relentless high-speed combat against a mist-transforming Bone Mecha, and ends with Oni Samurai slicing through the enemy’s torso and core, then physically pulverizing it with a devastating follow-up kick.

CHARACTER REFERENCES

IMAGE 1 — BONE MECHA:
Use the primary standing character from Image 1: ivory skull and exposed ribs fused with black machinery, glowing red eyes and chest core, spiked circular halo behind the head, torn crimson cloth, and the reference-matching red-edged polearm. Preserve its proportions, silhouette, and mechanical details. Ignore alternate character variations.
IMAGE 2 — ONI SAMURAI:
Use the character from Image 2: horned red oni mask, weathered red-and-black mechanical armor, exposed machinery, and katana. Preserve its face, armor construction, proportions, and weapon design.

Extract only these two characters from the reference sheets. Exclude sheet backgrounds, diagrams, labels, and duplicate turnaround figures. Maintain consistent designs throughout, with intentional combat damage occurring during the final destruction.
MOVEMENT AND ABILITY RULES

Use explosive acceleration, abrupt braking, rapid directional changes, airborne collisions, wall rebounds, and immediate counterattacks. Every impact triggers the next movement. Each one-second beat advances the fight through a clear attack, evasion, collision, or reversal.

Bone Mecha alone can transform into mist. Its body and equipment collapse into a narrow, fast-moving stream of ash-white vapor with crimson embers, travel along a continuous visible trajectory, and reconstruct already attacking. Transformations take approximately 0.1–0.2 seconds. Keep mist movement sharp, directional, and extremely fast.

Oni Samurai always remains solid. It moves through powerful dashes, explosive jumps, wall kicks, and physical recoil.

During the final finishing sequence, severing Bone Mecha’s chest core disables its mist transformation. From that moment onward, its body stays solid until physically shattered.

SETTING

A deserted Japanese city with wet asphalt, concrete buildings, glass facades, cold ambient light, and red reflections from the fighters. Maintain consistent street geography. Damage persists: broken walls remain broken, debris continues falling, and weakened buildings collapse after their supports are destroyed.

SECOND-BY-SECOND ACTION AND CAMERA

00:00–00:01 — FIRST BUILDING IMPACT
At the very first frame, Oni Samurai is ALREADY flying backward at extreme speed from Bone Mecha’s strike. Its armored back punches through a concrete building almost immediately. Walls fracture at the contact point and columns snap along its trajectory.
CAMERA: low, wide-angle lateral pursuit. The camera briefly lags behind the acceleration, then catches up as concrete fragments race across the foreground.

00:01–00:02 — SECOND BUILDING PENETRATION
Oni bursts out of the first building and immediately crashes through the next building’s facade and supporting columns. Keep its red armored silhouette readable between impacts. Structural collapse begins behind its flight path.
CAMERA: continue the same fast pursuit through the debris, with strong parallax and a brief directional jolt at the second impact.

00:02–00:03 — INSTANT REVERSAL
Oni strikes the road feet-first, briefly anchors its katana into the asphalt, pivots around the contact point, and launches back toward the attacker. Sparks trace a short, violent braking trench. The landing becomes an immediate countercharge.
CAMERA: cut on the landing to a ground-skimming tracking shot that follows the sudden reversal.
00:03–00:04 — MIST INTERCEPTION
Bone Mecha races through falling slabs as a tight stream of mist, reconstructs directly in Oni’s path, and immediately thrusts its polearm. Oni deflects the weapon while charging past it. Sparks burst between their moving bodies.
CAMERA: surge into a close tracking shot, keeping both fighters and the weapon contact visible.
00:04–00:05 — RAPID CUT AND COUNTER
Oni delivers two compact, lightning-fast sword cuts. Bone Mecha dissolves just before the first blade contact, shifts sideways, and reconstructs into a counterstrike as the second cut passes.
CAMERA: a short, fast arc follows the changing attack angle. Briefly stabilize at the clash so the contact remains readable.

00:05–00:06 — REAR ATTACK AND LAUNCHING KICK
Bone Mecha streams along Oni’s katana and curls behind its shoulder. Oni immediately pivots into a spinning heel kick, catching the enemy’s torso exactly as it becomes solid and launching it upward.
CAMERA: whip-pan with the mist, clearly catch the heel connecting, then snap upward after the launched enemy.
00:06–00:07 — VERTICAL PURSUIT
Oni compresses its legs and explosively jumps after Bone Mecha. The pavement fractures beneath its feet. It catches the enemy above street level and attacks before the enemy can recover.
CAMERA: accelerate vertically alongside both fighters as the buildings rush downward around them.
00:07–00:08 — AIRBORNE EXCHANGE
Show three rapid, distinct aerial weapon contacts: slash, parry, counterstrike. Both fighters change height and position after each collision, recoil, then immediately accelerate back together.
CAMERA: track beside the exchange with both fighters in frame and the street clearly visible far below.

00:08–00:09 — THROWN TOWARD THE WALL
Bone Mecha drives a diagonal polearm strike into Oni’s guard, throwing it sideways toward a building. Oni twists in midair, bringing both feet toward the approaching facade.
CAMERA: rapidly pull back to reveal the distance, then pan with the sideways flight.
00:09–00:10 — EXPLOSIVE WALL REBOUND
Oni plants both feet against the building and immediately wall-kicks back into combat. Glass and masonry burst from the launch point. It reaches Bone Mecha before the wall debris begins falling.
CAMERA: skim close to the facade, then swing outward along Oni’s rebound trajectory.
00:10–00:11 — DIVING COUNTERATTACK
Bone Mecha dissolves around the incoming katana, whips above Oni as a thin ribbon of mist, and reconstructs into a downward polearm strike. Oni turns its blade upward to intercept as both descend toward the street.
CAMERA: pitch downward into a steep descending pursuit, keeping the approaching ground visible.

00:11–00:12 — GROUND COLLISION AND OPENING
The fighters collide at street level, their weapons meeting for an instant. The landing fractures the asphalt and launches a circular wave of dust and debris. Oni immediately steps through the collision and knocks the polearm away from the enemy’s centerline.
CAMERA: finish the descent at waist height and continue laterally through the expanding debris, revealing the opening in Bone Mecha’s guard.
00:12–00:13 — DECISIVE TORSO AND CORE CUT
Bone Mecha commits to one final fully solid attack. Oni pivots inside its reach and delivers an explosive diagonal katana slash THROUGH THE ENEMY’S TORSO AND RED CHEST CORE.
Clearly show the blade contacting the solid ribs and mechanical core. The torso separates along the diagonal cut, and the two rigid sections visibly shift apart. The severed core flickers and loses power, disabling mist transformation.
CAMERA: move into a clear three-quarter medium shot. Keep the katana, torso, and visible separation in frame. Reveal the cut briefly while the action continues at full speed.

00:13–00:14 — PHYSICAL PULVERIZATION
Without pausing, Oni converts the slash’s rotational momentum into a devastating spinning side kick. Its armored heel drives directly into the already severed torso.
Show solid heel-to-body contact FIRST. Then shatter the enemy outward from that contact point: ivory bone fragments, broken ribs, black mechanical components, splintered halo segments, and pieces of the destroyed core erupt across the street. Large, distinct fragments appear before the finer dust expands.
CAMERA: follow the rotation with a short controlled arc, catch the kick in a readable side view, then rapidly pull back with the outward burst of debris. Add one sharp impact jolt.
00:14–00:15 — COMPLETE DEFEAT
Bone Mecha’s remaining structure breaks apart into tumbling bone and metal fragments. Its red eyes and chest core go completely dark. Heavy pieces strike the asphalt while smaller shards and sparks pass the lens.

Oni lands from the kick and completes its turning follow-through with the katana extended. The enemy’s shattered remains scatter across the street as damaged buildings continue crumbling behind it.
CAMERA: finish in a low, widening shot that clearly reveals Oni and the destroyed enemy. Keep the final frame alive with falling debris and the completion of Oni’s movement.
CINEMATOGRAPHY AND READABILITY
Treat the timestamps as connected choreography beats. Link adjacent beats with motivated tracking, whip-pans, short combat orbits, vertical pursuit, and descending camera movement.

Follow actual launches, strikes, dodges, and reversals. Preserve clear spatial relationships through direction changes. Use strong foreground parallax to convey speed and scale.
Keep motion blur directional. Preserve readable character silhouettes and sharp weapon contacts during key impacts. Camera shake is brief and tied to collisions. Keep dust, sparks, and debris from obscuring the decisive actions.
Maintain rapid motion throughout. Avoid extended preparation, hovering, prolonged weapon locks, static hero poses, slow mist travel, and prolonged slow motion.

ENDING REQUIREMENT
The final defeat is complete and irreversible. Clearly show this exact order:
BLADE CONTACT → TORSO AND CORE SEPARATION → FOLLOW-UP KICK CONTACT → FULL-BODY SHATTERING.
The final destruction consists of physical bone and machinery fragments. After the core is severed, Bone Mecha cannot turn into mist, regenerate, escape, or reappear. All remaining eye and core lights extinguish.

AUDIO
No music, dialogue, or narration. Begin with a powerful impact at the first frame. Use rapid directional air cracks, metallic weapon collisions, katana scraping asphalt, shattering glass, concrete fractures, and fast rushing mist.
For the finish, synchronize a sharp slicing crack, the heavy kick impact, brittle bone-and-metal fragmentation, and debris striking the street. End with falling fragments and deep structural collapse.

EXCLUDE
Extra fighters, duplicated bodies, additional limbs, accidental armor changes, floating weapons, unexplained teleportation, random camera spinning, excessive shake, smoke concealing the choreography, full-screen flashes hiding impacts, subtitles, titles, logos, and watermarks.
