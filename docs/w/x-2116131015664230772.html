Seedance 2.5 Prompt
Create a 30-second, 16:9, photorealistic cinematic action sequence.

STYLE
A powerful theatrical action film with explosive acceleration, heavy physical impacts, intricate mechanical transformation, and large-scale environmental destruction. Combine live-action realism with the extreme speed and impact of an anime battle. Real materials, convincing weight, sharp character silhouettes, dynamic daylight, orange firelight, and red-white lightning.

REFERENCE ASSIGNMENT
Reference Image 1: The completed armored warrior. Preserve the exact black, red, and ivory oni armor, horned helmet, demonic chest design, cables, proportions, and red Japanese katana designs. Approximately 3 meters tall. Use two reference-matching katanas charged with red-white electricity.

Reference Image 2: The enemy monster. Preserve its enormous curved black horns, long white hair, pale textured exterior, red chest marking, dark metal ornaments, and hoof-like feet. Approximately 3.2 meters tall throughout. Its organic-looking exterior covers a fully mechanical interior: armor, actuators, cables, and an internal power core. Damage reveals machinery, never flesh. It attacks with mouth-generated fire and extending mechanical claws. Do not use the scythe shown on its reference sheet.

Reference Image 3: The human rider before transformation. Preserve his face, dark skin, round sunglasses, black knit cap, short braids, black leather jacket, loose black trousers, and boots. Approximately 1.85 meters tall.

Reference Image 4: The motorcycle. Preserve its black, red, and ivory biomechanical design, oni front fairing, horns, thick tires, engine, and mounted katana components.

IDENTITY AND TRANSFORMATION CONTINUITY
There is one rider, one motorcycle, and one enemy.
The rider becomes the armored warrior by wearing the transformed motorcycle.
The human body does not grow. The motorcycle unfolds into a larger powered exoskeleton around him.
At the end of the transformation, the exterior matches Reference Image 1.
No separate rider, spare motorcycle, duplicate warrior, or leftover human body remains.

SETTING
Daytime inside a huge American shopping mall with a tall glass-roofed atrium, several retail levels, escalators, glass railings, furniture displays, and electronics storefronts.

The opening is packed with families and shoppers. As the attack begins, crowds flee into side corridors and exits, clearing the central combat area. Keep evacuation visible in the background and in the designated one-second inserts.

Outside, a multilevel highway interchange connects to the mall's elevated parking approach. Establish a clear route from the highway to the rooftop jump.

Preserve the mall's geography, accumulated fire, smoke, debris, and battle damage throughout.

CAMERA AND ACTION RULES
Every second introduces a clear action beat and purposeful camera movement.
Use low tracking shots, lateral pursuit, controlled whip pans, short push-ins, overhead views, and brief speed ramps.
Show the contact that causes each impact before showing the resulting destruction.
Keep the combatants readable during acceleration. Use background motion blur without turning the characters into indistinct streaks.
Keep the 9–12 second transformation visually continuous; do not conceal it with cuts, smoke, or a white flash.
Maintain clear screen direction and believable travel between levels.

SECOND-BY-SECOND SEQUENCE

00–01:
Black fog violently erupts between benches in the crowded central atrium. The horned monster from Image 2 steps out at its full, fixed size, scattering shopping bags with the pressure wave. A fast low-angle push reveals its horns and face through the fog.

01–02:
One-second human reaction shot. Shoppers scream and turn to flee; parents pull their children toward a side corridor. An eye-level camera retreats with the crowd while the monster remains visible behind them.

02–03:
The monster throws its head forward with a mechanical roar. Its mouth ignites and releases a powerful stream of orange fire toward an adjacent home-furnishing storefront. Track from its jaws along the visible fire stream.

03–04:
The same fire sweep crosses sofas, shelving, and a neighboring electronics display. Upholstery ignites, television screens burst, and appliances topple under the blast. A fast lateral tracking shot follows the destruction from its source.

04–05:
A wide, elevated shot reveals spreading storefront fires and large streams of families evacuating through peripheral corridors. Smoke rises toward the skylight while daylight still illuminates the atrium. The central floor begins to clear.

05–06:
Cut to the motorcycle from Image 4 already racing at extreme speed along a multilevel highway. A camera inches above the rear tire tracks its acceleration as overpasses sweep overhead and the smoking mall appears ahead.

06–07:
A close side-tracking shot clearly identifies the rider from Image 3. His sunglasses reflect the burning mall. He leans into the elevated exit leading onto the mall's parking approach, with wind pulling at his jacket.

07–08:
The motorcycle accelerates up a rising service ramp at the edge of the adjoining parking deck and launches toward the mall's glass roof. A wide side view clearly shows the ramp, airborne bike, gap, and skylight.

08–09:
The front wheel smashes through the skylight. Glass bursts inward as rider and motorcycle descend into the burning atrium. The camera follows through the opening, revealing the monster below and the now-cleared landing area.

09–10:
Begin a continuous airborne transformation shot. The motorcycle frame splits along precise mechanical seams. Powered leg structures unfold beneath and around the rider's legs; foot supports lock under his boots. His body remains intact and visibly centered inside the expanding exoskeleton.

10–11:
Without cutting, the camera moves into a close three-quarter tracking view. The oni front fairing folds into the chest armor. Engine and chassis components lock around the torso and spine. Wheel hubs compact into the circular shoulder mechanisms; remaining tire sections retract inside the armor. Forearm plates close around his arms.

11–12:
The same camera rises toward the rider's face. Helmet sections fold around his cap and glasses, then the horned oni faceplate snaps shut last. All remaining components lock into the exact silhouette of Image 1. Katana hilts settle at his hips. Use one brief speed ramp to emphasize the final mechanical lock.

12–13:
The fully armored warrior lands heavily on the cleared atrium floor, cracking tiles beneath both feet. He immediately draws both red katanas, igniting red-white electrical arcs. A low camera tracks across his landing into the approaching monster.

13–14:
The monster lunges and extends its claws toward the warrior's head. The warrior catches the claws between crossed katanas. Show the actual metal-on-metal contact, sparks, and compressed force in a close lateral shot.

14–15:
The warrior redirects the trapped claw and drives a lightning-charged slash across the monster's armored torso. The impact throws the monster backward through the glass frontage of the damaged electronics store. Whip-pan with its trajectory and reveal the collision.

15–16:
From inside the wrecked storefront, the monster fires a continuous horizontal flame jet. The warrior accelerates sideways along the concourse as the flame strikes the tiles and displays immediately behind him. Use fast lateral pursuit with strong foreground parallax.

16–17:
The monster bursts through its own smoke and extends a clawed arm into the warrior's path. A heavy claw strike catches the warrior's armor and launches him across the atrium toward a decorative column and an empty kiosk. Track the hit and its direction in one readable shot.

17–18:
The warrior crashes through the decorative column's cladding and the empty kiosk. Panels, counter fragments, and tiles burst outward. His armor remains recognizable with accumulating scratches. A low camera follows his sliding body through the wreckage.

18–19:
His armored feet bite into the floor, carving two trenches as he brakes. He plants one foot against the broken kiosk base and launches back toward the monster with both blades trailing narrow electrical arcs. The camera tracks beside his acceleration.

19–20:
Exactly one second of fleeing civilians. A family rushes through a side exit with other shoppers while red-white battle flashes illuminate the corridor behind them. A short handheld tracking shot conveys urgency; nobody is struck or visibly injured.

20–21:
Return to the same attack trajectory. The warrior slips inside the monster's extended claws and slices through the projecting metal talons. Show severed mechanical claw tips spinning away with sparks and cables, without blood. A tight side view keeps the cut readable.

21–22:
Continuing the combination, the warrior delivers a rising cross-slash that launches the monster onto the upper escalator landing. The monster crashes through the landing's glass railing. The camera tilts upward with its movement, establishing the new elevation.

22–23:
The monster digs its remaining claws into the upper landing to halt its slide, then unleashes a massive cone of fire downward toward the warrior. An over-the-shoulder view from behind the monster shows the hero below and the fire's trajectory.

23–24:
The warrior accelerates up the wall beside the escalator, staying just outside the flame cone. The fire tears through an empty connecting walkway behind him. A vertical tracking shot follows his wall run toward the monster's level.

24–25:
He kicks off the wall and crosses both electrified katanas in front of him. Their electrical field opens a brief channel through the fire as he bursts forward. A frontal tracking camera retreats ahead of his sharply visible armored silhouette.

25–26:
The warrior reaches the monster and delivers an X-shaped strike across the red chest marking. Pale exterior plates crack open, exposing mechanical ribs and the glowing power assembly beneath. A close three-quarter shot shows both blades making contact.

26–27:
He drives the katana tips into the exposed mechanical chest assembly and discharges an intense surge of red-white electricity. Arcs spread through the monster's joints, horns, and metal ornaments. Its internal machinery overloads visibly.

27–28:
The warrior withdraws both swords and delivers a powerful kick that sends the electrically overloaded monster off the upper landing into the evacuated center of the atrium. A wide shot clearly shows the separation and the monster's downward path.

28–29:
The monster strikes the central floor. Its overloaded core detonates in a huge electrical explosion, followed by a heavy orange fireball and a circular dust wave. The monster breaks into unmistakable mechanical scrap: armor plates, bent frames, cables, gears, and sparking components. No flesh or blood.

29–30:
Finish on a low three-quarter shot of the warrior holding position on the upper landing, both katanas lowered but still crackling. Behind him, the explosion subsides through the atrium and metal debris falls to the floor. The enemy is destroyed and does not reappear. End on the warrior's recognizable silhouette without a fade.

AUDIO
Original instrumental industrial-electronic action music with heavy drums, distorted bass, and escalating tension.
Build the music during the highway approach.
Synchronize metallic percussion with the airborne armor assembly.
Deliver a strong musical impact at the completed suit landing.
Drive the combat with forceful rhythmic momentum, then briefly thin the music before the final explosion.

Layer clear environmental and action sounds:
Crowd panic and nonverbal screams, mall reverberation, fire alarms, roaring flames, motorcycle acceleration, wind, breaking skylight glass, mechanical servos, armor locks, electrical crackle, katana impacts, extending claws, collapsing displays, concrete impacts, and the final explosion.

Keep critical transformation and impact sounds audible above the music.
No spoken dialogue, narration, or song lyrics.

EXCLUSIONS
No subtitles, captions, speech bubbles, title cards, reference-sheet labels, diagrams, or added graphic logos.
No blood, gore, organic dismemberment, or injured civilians.
No character duplication, identity swapping, random size changes, or regeneration of destroyed scenery.
No independent robot appearing beside the rider.
No motorcycle remaining after the transformation.
No liquid-metal morphing, magical body growth, or unexplained armor appearing from nowhere.
No black-fog teleportation during combat; the fog is used only for the monster's initial arrival.
No prolonged slow motion, repeated posing, unreadable camera spinning, or effects that hide decisive actions.
