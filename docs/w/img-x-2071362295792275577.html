"Isolate each individual frame from the contact sheet, then recreate every frame as a separate high-resolution [aspect ratio e.g 16:9/21:9] image.

Preserve the original composition, framing, lighting, colour palette, subject placement, atmosphere, and cinematic style exactly. Do not redesign, reinterpret, crop awkwardly, or introduce new details.

Preserve the exact AESTHETIC, COLOUR PALETTE, LIGHTING, MOOD, SUBJECT DESIGN, and ENVIRONMENT from the source image.

Each extracted frame should feel like a polished standalone film still at the highest possible resolution."
