Prompt :

[Global Setting] 30-second cinematic scene, 16:9, 4K, photoreal, post-apocalyptic. A ruined funfair at sunset under a colorless sky. Heavily desaturated, almost black and white. The ONLY saturated color in the entire video is one red foam clown nose. No dialogue, no music, no text on screen.

[Character] A lone courier: worn hooded coat, cracked gas mask, a broken radio strapped to the chest. Moves slowly.

[0–7s] Wide shot. The courier walks through the dead carnival. A frozen ferris wheel against the pale sky. Rotting prize booths hanging open. Gray stuffed animals dangling, soaked by rain. Weeds pushing through painted concrete.

[7–13s] Tracking shot past peeling clown murals, their painted faces fading into blank surfaces. A loose game token rolls across the ground.

[13–19s] Behind them, a carousel horse creaks forward on its own, slowly, without music. The courier turns, motionless.

[19–26s] Inside a collapsed booth, the courier finds a cracked souvenir case. Through the glass: one bright red foam clown nose, vivid against the gray world. They pick it up.

[26–30s] Close-up: the red nose resting in the courier's gloved palm, held like precious proof. Hold.

[Camera] Slow, patient, steady movements. Wide shots, then intimate close-ups. No cuts.

[Audio] Wind, distant creaking metal, broken radio static, the token rolling, the carousel's slow creak, glass cracking, then quiet wind.

[Consistency] Same courier. Everything gray except the red nose. Patient dread, dead carnival wonder.
