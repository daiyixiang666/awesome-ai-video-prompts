A Summer Night at the Dinner Table 👇

Inside a traditional Japanese farmhouse, two young friends turn an ordinary dinner into a memory they'll never forget. A stolen bite of grilled fish sparks a playful chopstick battle, laughter fills the room, and the evening ends with a simple toast and a shared strawberry. Shot with nostalgic handheld framing, warm lantern light, vintage film texture, and the gentle atmosphere of a classic Japanese slice-of-life film.
