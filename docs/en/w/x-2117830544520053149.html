Prompt · from the author's reply

Create a 30-second ultra-realistic live-action countryside vlog set in a peaceful Japanese village in 1998. Make it look like authentic family footage recorded on a Sony MiniDV camcorder.

CHARACTERS & LOCATION:
A young Japanese woman with loosely tied dark hair, wearing a cream blouse, light blue jeans and brown sandals. Her elderly grandmother has short grey hair and wears a floral house dress with an apron. Keep their faces, outfits and appearance consistent.

Location: Traditional Japanese farmhouse with shoji doors, wooden floors, vegetable garden, chicken coop, veranda, rice fields and quiet village roads.

EXACT SCENE SEQUENCE:

1. WAKING UP (0–3s):
The girl wakes up on a futon as warm morning sunlight enters through the window. She sits up and stretches.

2. GRANDMA'S GREETING (3–5s):
Grandma opens the bedroom door, smiles and invites her to breakfast. The girl follows her.

3. GARDEN HARVEST (5–8s):
The girl picks tomatoes, cucumbers, eggplants and green onions into a woven basket while grandma works nearby.

4. FRESH EGGS (8–10s):
She visits the chicken coop, collects two fresh eggs and carefully places them in her basket.

5. WATERING PLANTS (10–12s):
She waters the vegetable garden while grandma tends the flowers.

6. COOKING TOGETHER (12–17s):
Inside the traditional kitchen, they prepare miso soup, rice, grilled fish and tamagoyaki. Show washing, chopping, steam and sizzling food.

7. HOMEMADE LUNCH (17–20s):
They sit at a low wooden table. Grandma serves her granddaughter extra food, and they happily eat together.

8. VILLAGE WALK (20–23s):
The girl walks along a quiet village road past green rice fields, wooden houses and local villagers.

9. EVENING TEA (23–26s):
She sits with grandma on the wooden veranda, sharing green tea while enjoying the gentle evening breeze.

10. SUNSET ENDING (26–30s):
The girl helps grandma prepare dinner. End with a beautiful exterior shot of their warmly lit farmhouse at sunset.

VISUAL STYLE:
Photorealistic live-action, authentic 1998 Sony MiniDV camcorder footage, subtle grain, faded warm colors, imperfect handheld framing, soft focus and natural exposure shifts. Realistic expressions, movements and Japanese countryside details.

Natural ambient sounds only: birds, chickens, footsteps, cooking and village atmosphere. No modern objects, smartphones, animation, anime, teleportation or sudden scene changes.

Maintain strict character, outfit and location consistency. Show every scene in the exact order. The final video should feel like a nostalgic family recording from 1998.
