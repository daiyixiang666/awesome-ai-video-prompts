Prompt · from the post

Inside a colossal Japanese-inspired fortress suspended in darkness, endless wooden halls with shoji screens, torii-like beams, tatami floors, and layered staircases stack vertically in impossible symmetry. The Hyperspeed FPV camera drops instantly into a vertical free fall, accelerating brutally through the core of the structure. Lantern-lit corridors slide past sideways, staircases invert, and entire rooms rotate as the camera descends. A fast but controlled spin blends into the fall, twisting red paper lanterns, carved wooden pillars, and sliding doors into precise cinematic spirals while preserving spatial clarity. The environment feels heavy and real—aged wood textures, rice paper walls flexing in the wind, massive support beams rushing inches from the lens. Motion blur remains intense yet readable, anchored by warm lantern light and deep shadows. Audio is grounded and immersive: roaring wind, creaking timber, sharp air-cutting whooshes synced to acceleration, and a dark cinematic score with Japanese-inspired percussion driving relentless momentum. Realistic lighting, dramatic contrast, epic scale, one-shot cinematic realism
