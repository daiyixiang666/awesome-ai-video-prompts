Prompt · from the post

Create a 30-second ultra-realistic cinematic short film with a mysterious, surreal sci-fi atmosphere.

A vast abandoned salt plain stretches beneath a pale stormy sky. Far in the distance, enormous rusted metal structures rise from the ground like the remains of a forgotten civilization. Thin streams of glowing dust move through the air against the wind, creating an unnatural sense that time itself is behaving incorrectly.

A young woman in a simple charcoal-grey coat, dark trousers, worn leather boots, and a faded scarf walks alone across the empty landscape. She carries a small vintage mechanical compass in one hand. The camera follows her slowly from behind with natural handheld movement and subtle environmental motion.

At around 7 seconds, she notices something strange: her footprints behind her are gradually disappearing, one by one, as though the ground is erasing her past. She stops. The wind becomes almost completely silent.

She looks at the compass. Its needle spins rapidly, then suddenly points directly toward one of the distant structures.

She walks closer. As she approaches, the environment begins subtly changing around her. The sky shifts from daylight to twilight for a few seconds, then returns to daylight. Her scarf briefly moves in slow motion while everything else remains normal. Keep the transformation physically believable and understated rather than fantastical.

Cut to an intimate close-up of her face. She looks frightened but composed, realizing she has experienced this moment before. Tiny particles float past her eyes.

She slowly turns toward the camera. Behind her, the enormous structure has completely changed position without making any sound.

She gives a very slight, almost knowing smile.

Final shot: she walks toward the structure as the camera pulls far upward, revealing hundreds of identical footprints forming a massive circular pattern across the salt plain.

Photorealistic live-action cinematography, realistic human performance, natural skin texture, physically believable environments, muted grey and earth tones, soft overcast illumination, atmospheric depth, subtle film grain, restrained visual effects, realistic wind and dust simulation, cinematic lens compression, slow controlled camera movements, premium feature-film aesthetic.

Maintain consistent character identity, clothing, environment, and proportions throughout. No text, no subtitles, no logos, no artificial-looking CGI, no exaggerated fantasy effects, no montage feeling. The entire video should feel like one continuous mysterious cinematic event unfolding in real time.
