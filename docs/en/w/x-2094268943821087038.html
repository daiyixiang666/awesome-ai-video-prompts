Last Meeting on New Year's Eve

On a small riverside bridge on New Year's Eve, a girl in glasses smiles through tears as he gently wipes them away, then the two walk off separately under fireworks.

Prompt · from the post

A quiet riverside pedestrian path or small neighborhood bridge on New Year’s Eve at night.
This is their final meeting before parting. Character A still loves Character B but knows she must leave. Emotional arc:
plea → failed smile → fragile affection → gentle cheek touch and tear wipe → immediate rejection → broken farewell → hurried departure
Character B speaks only once. Character A speaks only once.

SHOT 1 — 0s–1.2s | CLOSE-UP / PLEA
Tight close-up on Character A. Character B’s blurred shoulder remains in the right foreground.
 From the blurred foreground, Character B softly says in Japanese:
「行かないで。」
 (“Don’t go.”)
The line is quiet, intimate, and restrained.
 Character A tries to smile, but it fails almost immediately. Her eyes are wet and deeply sad. She lowers her gaze slightly.
Show:
• wet lower eyelids
• tiny lower-lip tremble
• uneven inhale
• restrained heartbreak

SHOT 2 — 1.2s–3.8s | CLOSE-UP / FRAGILE COMPOSURE
Remain in a tight emotional close-up. Character A looks back up and attempts another weaker, apologetic smile. Her eyes communicate affection, regret, and finality. She releases a tiny breathy exhale that almost becomes a laugh but breaks. She swallows, glances down briefly, then raises her gaze again.
No dialogue.

SHOT 3 — 3.8s–6.4s | GENTLE TOUCH / TEAR WIPE / REJECTION
Character B slowly raises one hand from the blurred foreground and gently touches Character A’s left cheek while softly wiping away her tears.
The moment his fingers touch:
• her breath stops
• eyes widen slightly
• fragile smile disappears
• jaw tightens
• body tenses
• more tears gather
She does not lean into the touch. After a brief beat, Character A immediately raises one hand, touches his wrist or outer hand, and firmly but carefully brushes his hand away in one clean motion.
Sequence must be clear:
gentle cheek touch → tear wipe → emotional shock → immediate rejection → his hand lowers
No slap. No aggression. No repeated touch.

SHOT 4 — 6.4s–8.2s | MEDIUM CLOSE-UP / FINAL LINE
Character A takes two quick steps backward. Camera gently pulls back to a medium close-up while keeping her sharply focused. Character B remains blurred on the right.
She looks at him with wet eyes and softly says in Japanese:
「元気でね。」
 (“Take care.”)
Delivery: quiet, broken, intimate, almost crying.
 After speaking, her lips stay slightly parted. She drops her gaze and lightly grips part of her clothing or closes one hand near her side to steady herself.

SHOT 5 — 8.2s–10s | LONG SHOT / HURRIED DEPARTURE
Cut to a long shot. Character A suddenly turns away and leaves quickly, as if she must escape before breaking down. She moves rapidly along the riverside path. Her steps are hurried and uneven, hair and clothing moving naturally. She briefly raises one hand near her mouth or chest. She never looks back.
Character B remains behind as a blurred, motionless figure. He does not follow.
End while she is still leaving.
