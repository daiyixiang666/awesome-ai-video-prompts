Prompt · from the post

A Caravaggio-style oil painting with stark chiaroscuro against a deep black background. Two elderly fishermen in tattered clothing haul a dripping net up from a wooden boat. Tangled inside is a struggling pelican, its beak and one wing thrust out through the gaps in the mesh. The rope netting binds its body and pulls taut between the men’s hands. One shaft of light from the upper left strikes the pelican’s white plumage and the fishermen’s faces.
